### 💬 How I work

> I don't treat dashboards as the finish line — the useful part is the "so what."
> In the Contoso project, the real deliverable wasn't the segmentation table, it was the recommendation: stop trying to grow the low-value segment and instead focus on retaining the top 12K customers, because losing even a few of them would hurt more than growing the customer count ever could.
>
> That's generally how I approach a dataset: find the pattern, quantify it, then say what a business should actually *do* about it.
